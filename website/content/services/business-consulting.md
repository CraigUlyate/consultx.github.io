## What we do

ConsultX provides practical business consulting that connects commercial reality to execution. We help owners and leadership teams clarify priorities, pressure-test plans, improve profitability and make better investment or restructuring decisions.

This is hands-on advisory — grounded in numbers, operations and South African business context — not abstract strategy decks that never leave the boardroom.

## How we create value

- Sharper priorities and a clearer commercial roadmap
- Better decisions on pricing, cost, growth and capital allocation
- Stronger alignment between strategy, finance and operations
- Practical recommendations your team can implement
- An independent view when internal capacity or perspective is limited

## How we work

1. **Scope** — Define the decision, problem or opportunity with clear success criteria.
2. **Analyse** — Review financials, operations, market context and management insight.
3. **Recommend** — Present options with trade-offs, risks and expected impact.
4. **Support** — Help leadership action the plan through focused follow-through.

## Who it’s for

Founders, executives and boards who need structured thinking on growth, turnaround, investment readiness, commercial performance or organisational change — with advice that is commercially literate and execution-minded.
