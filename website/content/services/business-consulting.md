## What we do

ConsultX provides practical business consulting that connects commercial reality to execution. We help owners and leadership teams clarify priorities, pressure-test plans, improve profitability and make better investment or restructuring decisions.

A core focus is corporate finance efficiency: we help organisations cut existing finance operating costs by up to 50% through clearer process design, task automation and stronger control. This is hands-on advisory — grounded in numbers, operations and South African business context — not abstract strategy decks that never leave the boardroom.

## How we create value

- Cut corporate finance operating costs by up to 50% with a clear commercial case
- Sharper priorities and a solution roadmap after an initial assessment
- ERP-agnostic recommendations focused on task automation, not forced system replacement
- Rigorous controls and checks built into redesigned ways of working
- Better decisions on pricing, cost, growth and capital allocation
- Practical recommendations your team can implement

## How we work

1. **Assess** — Perform an initial assessment of the decision, cost base, process landscape and success criteria.
2. **Roadmap** — Provide a solution roadmap with sequenced initiatives, trade-offs and expected impact.
3. **Recommend** — Present options spanning process redesign, task automation and operating-model change — ERP agnostic.
4. **Support** — Help leadership action the plan with rigorous controls, checks and focused follow-through.

## Who it’s for

Founders, executives and boards who need structured thinking on growth, turnaround, finance cost reduction, investment readiness or organisational change — with advice that is commercially literate and execution-minded.
