## What we do

ConsultX prepares independent business valuations for transactions, reporting, shareholder matters, litigation support and strategic planning. We combine financial analysis, market context and professional judgement to produce clear, defensible valuation outcomes.

Valuations are purpose-led: the standard of value, assumptions and report depth are tailored to why the valuation is needed.

## How we create value

- A credible view of business value for buyers, sellers and shareholders
- Transparent assumptions and methods that stand up to scrutiny
- Support for deal negotiations, exits, buy-ins and restructuring
- Scenario insight that shows how value moves with key drivers
- Professional reporting suitable for boards, advisors and counterparties

## How we work

1. **Brief** — Confirm purpose, standard of value, timeline and information needs.
2. **Analyse** — Assess historical performance, forecasts, risk and market context.
3. **Value** — Apply appropriate methods such as DCF and market-based approaches.
4. **Report** — Deliver a clear valuation conclusion with supporting analysis.

## Who it’s for

Business owners, investors, boards and advisors needing an independent valuation for sale/purchase discussions, shareholder agreements, internal restructuring, financing conversations or strategic decision-making.
