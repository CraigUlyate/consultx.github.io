## What we do

ConsultX helps you build a finance function that is accurate, timely and useful. We strengthen the foundations — charts of accounts, close routines, controls, cash visibility and management reporting — so leadership can trust the numbers and act with confidence.

Whether you are scaling, cleaning up after rapid growth, or preparing for investment, we design finance processes that match the size and complexity of your business.

## How we create value

- Faster, cleaner month-end closes with fewer surprises
- Clearer cash and performance visibility for owners and boards
- Stronger internal controls that reduce leakage and audit friction
- Reporting packs that support decisions, not just compliance
- A finance operating rhythm your team can sustain

## How we work

1. **Diagnose** — Review current reporting, close process, systems and control gaps.
2. **Design** — Define the target finance operating model, KPIs and reporting cadence.
3. **Implement** — Improve processes, templates, reconciliations and handovers.
4. **Embed** — Train the team and leave behind a repeatable monthly rhythm.

## Who it’s for

Growing SMEs, founder-led businesses and finance teams that need more structure without unnecessary complexity — especially where reporting is late, inconsistent, or not trusted by decision-makers.
