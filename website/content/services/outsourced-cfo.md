## What we do

Our Outsourced CFO service gives you senior finance leadership without the cost of a full-time executive. ConsultX steps in to own or co-own the finance agenda — cash, forecasting, reporting, board packs, lender packs and decision support — while working alongside your existing bookkeeper, accountant or finance team.

You get strategic finance capacity that flexes with the business.

## How we create value

- CFO-level insight at a fraction of permanent employment cost
- Stronger cash planning, forecasting and board-ready reporting
- Better financial discipline for growth, funding or turnaround phases
- A bridge between day-to-day accounting and executive decision-making
- Continuity and calm when internal finance capacity is stretched

## How we work

1. **Engage** — Agree scope, cadence, stakeholders and reporting expectations.
2. **Stabilise** — Get cash, reporting and close routines onto a reliable footing.
3. **Lead** — Drive forecasts, packs, reviews and priority finance initiatives.
4. **Scale** — Adjust involvement as the business grows or internal capacity improves.

## Who it’s for

Growing SMEs, founder-led companies and management teams that need senior finance leadership for a season — or on an ongoing fractional basis — without hiring a full-time CFO too early.
