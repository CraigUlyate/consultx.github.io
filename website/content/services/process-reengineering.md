## What we do

We redesign the way work moves through your finance and operations. ConsultX maps current processes, removes wasteful steps, clarifies ownership and builds cleaner handovers between people, systems and teams.

The goal is practical: shorter cycle times, fewer errors, less firefighting, and processes that scale as the business grows.

## How we create value

- Reduced rework, bottlenecks and manual chasing
- Clearer roles, approvals and accountability
- Faster invoice-to-cash, procure-to-pay and close cycles
- Better readiness for automation and system improvements
- Processes that are documented, trainable and auditable

## How we work

1. **Map** — Capture the current process end-to-end, including exceptions and workarounds.
2. **Simplify** — Remove duplication, clarify decisions and redesign the critical path.
3. **Standardise** — Define controls, SLAs, templates and system touchpoints.
4. **Enable** — Support rollout, training and monitoring so the new process sticks.

## Who it’s for

Businesses where growth has outpaced process maturity — common symptoms include delayed reporting, messy handovers, spreadsheet dependency, or teams spending too much time on admin instead of insight.
