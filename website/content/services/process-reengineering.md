## What we do

We redesign the way work moves through your finance and operations. ConsultX maps current processes, removes wasteful steps, clarifies ownership and builds cleaner handovers between people, systems and teams.

The goal is practical: shorter cycle times, fewer errors, less firefighting, and processes that scale as the business grows. In corporate finance environments, this work is aimed at cutting existing operating costs by up to 50% — without weakening control.

## How we create value

- Cut corporate finance operating costs by up to 50% through leaner process design
- Reduced rework, bottlenecks and manual chasing
- Clearer roles, approvals and accountability with rigorous controls and checks
- Faster invoice-to-cash, procure-to-pay and close cycles
- Better readiness for task automation — ERP agnostic, so gains are not locked to one system
- Processes that are documented, trainable and auditable

## How we work

1. **Assess** — Perform an initial assessment of current finance and operational processes, cost drivers and control gaps.
2. **Roadmap** — Provide a solution roadmap that sequences quick wins and structural redesign.
3. **Simplify** — Remove duplication, clarify decisions and redesign the critical path.
4. **Standardise & automate** — Define controls, SLAs and task automation opportunities that work across your existing ERP and tools.
5. **Enable** — Support rollout, training and monitoring so the new process sticks.

## Who it’s for

Businesses where growth has outpaced process maturity — common symptoms include delayed reporting, messy handovers, spreadsheet dependency, or teams spending too much time on admin instead of insight.
